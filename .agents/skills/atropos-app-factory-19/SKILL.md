# Atropos App Factory 19

## Procedure

1. Read `references/app-factory-19.md`.
2. Convert the request into a bounded plan before generating files.
3. Produce code, verify it, and prove the artifact runs or installs.
4. Accept only after the artifact, smoke, and handoff are truthful.

## Guardrails

- Do not accept a request that lacks artifact proof.
- Do not mix app-factory work with phase 20 autonomy work.
- Keep screenshot smoke or install/run proof tied to the exact artifact slice.
