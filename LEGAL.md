# Legal and procurement note

ATROPOS is distributed under the AGPL-3.0-only license. The planner,
provider routing, policy gates, bridge, client protocol, local evidence store,
and governance ledger are open repository components; optional hosted services
are additive and do not replace local authority.

Operators distributing a modified network service should review the applicable
AGPL section 13 source-availability obligations with qualified legal counsel.
This note is informational, is not legal advice, and does not replace the
authoritative text in [LICENSE](LICENSE) or the detailed
[open-core boundary](docs/OPEN_CORE_BOUNDARY.md).
